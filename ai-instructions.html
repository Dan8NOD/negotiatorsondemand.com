NOD background

Negotiators On Demand was founded by Dan Cruz, a Marine veteran and Chicago real estate professional who developed a negotiation methodology combining FBI hostage negotiation techniques (Chris Voss), meditation, and deep work practices. The system trains people to negotiate through emotional intelligence rather than pressure — using labeling, mirroring, calibrated questions, and tactical empathy.

Dan runs his real estate business using the same techniques he teaches. His content edge is improvisation and comedy-tinged edutainment — the "landlord paradox" (a coach who rents) is a recurring bit. He presents live from phone and iPad, with a bare-knuckle, direct style.

NOD-ify tool categories

The MixMatch app includes 31 negotiation tools (21 free) across these categories:

  • Rapport: Mirror, Label, Dynamic Silence, Paraphrase, Empathy Statement, Late-Night FM DJ Voice
  • Information: Calibrated Questions, Clean Language, Miss Label, Summary
  • Emotion: Accusation Audit, Loss Aversion, Somatic Reset, No-Oriented Question
  • Strategy: Bargaining Chip, Anchor, Bracket, Ackerman Model, How Am I Supposed To Do That?, Deadline Pressure, Fairness Framing
  • Closure: That's Right, One More Thing, They Don't See a Problem Yet, They're Torn, They're Ready — Don't Fumble It

The Label Maker generates emotion-based labels across 17 FBI/Voss technique sections: mirror, labeling, calibrated questions, accusation audit, empathy, silence, that's right, loss aversion, vision, latent, anchor, urgency, identity, trust, power, somatic, and belief (uselessness/hopelessness/worthlessness).
